# 01: Coverage journal: Overview, spell pages and the pack-content test

**What to build:** A player opens the new **Spell and Ability Coverage** journal and finds every SRD spell listed with
its treatment: Automated, Assisted or Manual. Each row links the spell, says what the system calculates and gives the
concrete manual steps for the rest. The Overview page defines the three treatments, says a human always decides outcome,
recipient and application, and records the reclassifications and source ambiguities. Remove one spell's row from the page
source and the tests fail, naming the spell. First slice of chunk **Cov**; see [the spec](../spec.md), section "Coverage
treatments and the coverage page", the "Pack-content test" invariants and user stories 1, 3–10, 12.

The page states **current shipped behaviour**: until a spell's batch lands its row says Manual, and the batch flips the
row in its own PR. Rows for templates a mechanic ticket has already shipped (Protection, Shield, Weapon Enhance, Enhance
Skill, Vigour) state their real treatment. The journal is authored as HTML with the other documentation journals and
generated by the docs build. Before writing the rows, confirm that journal HTML keeps its table structure through the
docs build, since the row contract depends on it.

This ticket covers only the invariants that need no effect data: rows, treatments, item ids and item icons. Template
invariants and worked examples come in ticket 03, because the change vocabulary doesn't exist on day one.
