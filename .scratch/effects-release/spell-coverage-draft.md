# Spell coverage inventory

Supporting evidence for [Choose effect mechanics and the spell/ability coverage boundary](issues/01-supported-mechanics.md).
The resolved mechanics ticket owns the authoritative scope and operation decisions. This inventory applies that boundary
to content; per-template details, UI and implementation order still need their own review. No spell behavior is implemented.

## Corpus and rubric

The local `src/packs/spells/` corpus contains **165 YAML documents: 162 spell items and 3 folder documents**.
The tables enumerate every pack spell item exactly once: **71 Personal Magic, 53 Divine Magic, 38 Sorcery**.
They additionally include the two genuine SRD spells missing from the pack, clearly marked **source-only**.
The full local SRD has **163 actual spell entries: 70 Personal Magic, 54 Divine Magic, 39 Sorcery**.
The union inventory has **164 rows** because the pack also represents the Binding Spirits procedure as a spell item.
The three folders are listed separately after the tables, so every YAML document is accounted for exactly once.
Parameterized spells count as their single existing template, not every possible learned variant.
Babel lacks `system.type`; its Personal Magic grouping follows its existing parent folder. This is a source-data observation,
not a requested code/content correction.

Coverage treatment terms (derived from the mechanics decision):

- **Automated**: after a human decides the outcome, recipient, magnitude/allocation and application, an unambiguous ongoing numeric modifier recomputes while the effect is active. Actual damage/healing and applicability/stacking judgments stay human unless separately agreed. This is proposed capability, not a statement of existing support.
- **Assisted**: a numeric subset combines with human judgments or additional manual consequences. This includes conditional numeric effects selected from matching entries in the test roll dialog; the human judges relevance and the system calculates the selected contribution.
- **Manual**: rules text, reminder and existing roll/resource/scene controls provide the workflow; no ongoing actor numeric modifier is assumed. Manual can involve numbers, such as explicit HP/MP edits, damage reduction adjudication or a separate ledger. Supported conditional test modifiers instead use the Assisted workflow.

Human application and ending/removal are the baseline for all rows. No automatic opposed rolls, next-applicable-use consumption,
damage adjudication, entity creation, region triggers or hidden-information disclosure is assumed. Duration handling is a separate
decision: a numeric row does not promise automatic expiration. Numeric proposals must preserve learned/unmodified skill and Master
semantics, and apply the eventual agreed modifier-combination policy. A manual spell need not produce an actor effect when a one-time
action and the existing rule card suffice.

Conditional effects use explicit per-test selection, not automatic context detection or a permanent activation toggle.
Only conditional effects matching the current test are listed; unselected entries do not contribute. Direct references
in a skill's own formula determine characteristic eligibility, with no recursive inference or stored fallback basis.
Next-use consumption remains manual even when the numeric bonus is offered as a conditional choice.

The [resolved mechanics decision](issues/01-supported-mechanics.md) records maximum/current coupling and the
Enhance/Diminish POW exception. Spending, lower bounds, negative changes, overflow and special pools remain with the
[resource decision](issues/02-resource-semantics.md). Enchantment rows describe their minimum manual workflow; later
resource/enchantment decisions may extend assistance without changing the underlying rules.

Planned treatments for the **163 actual SRD spells**: **14 automated, 28 assisted, 121 manual**.
Including the pack-only Binding Spirits procedure gives **164 inventory rows: 14 automated, 28 assisted, 122 manual**.

## Mechanically important exceptions for the decision

- **Separate learned skill, test modifier and combat order.** Enhance Skill, Coordination, Haste, Hamper, Fanaticism and Weapon Enhance target different values. A raised test value must not become Master status; Haste/Hamper must not change rolled skill. Coordination/Strength use matching conditional dialog entries for Athletics; the person rolling chooses whether the named characteristic governs this use.
- **Characteristic changes need real dependency recalculation.** Enhance/Diminish POW explicitly do not change magic points. The resolved decision preserves both MP maximum and current MP for Enhance/Diminish POW; direct MP-max and direct/indirect HP-max changes use the agreed current-value delta. Diminish has floor 1; Mindblast has no stated floor. Shapechange replaces four characteristics and changes anatomy; its magnitude example conflicts with the stated threshold and needs human adjudication rather than an invented algorithm.
- **Damage components matter.** True Weapon doubles normal weapon dice only; Dull Weapon leaves wielder Damage Modifier intact; Strength damage is nonmagical; Weapon Enhance adds magical damage to nonmagical base damage; Fireblade adds fire damage while Firearrow's description states a 1D10 fire hit without the same explicit additive wording. A single whole-roll multiply or magical flag loses these distinctions.
- **Temporary HP is a pool.** Vigour's magical HP absorb damage first and vanish with the spell. A generic HP-max increase does not preserve wounded ordinary HP and absorbed damage. Tap is a permanent characteristic change plus an explicit MP ledger with cap/decay, not an ordinary reversible effect.
- **One-time mutations stay explicit.** Heal, Divine Heal, Remove Disease, Treat Wounds, Restore Energy, Sap Energy, damage and death spells are not recurring actor preparation changes. Cure-specific restoration must not heal unrelated wounds or restore unrelated characteristic loss.
- **A resistance success can still leave an effect.** Fear and Madness have shaken −20% all-tests branches on ordinary Persistence success; critical success grants temporary same-spell immunity. Fumbles and failures have different durations, behaviors and resource consequences. The existing rule tables are needed in addition to spell descriptions.
- **Counters differ.** Countermagic ends after its first attempt regardless of success; Spell Resistance persists and also blocks beneficial spells; Damage Resistance ignores qualifying whole hits rather than reducing every hit. Dispel/Dismiss stop at an unremovable spell, while Neutralise can skip a too-strong spell. These distinctions favor explicit GM handling over a general dispel/ward engine.
- **Stacking is rule-specific.** Shield says its benefits are cumulative and lists incompatible defenses; Fanaticism/Demoralise cancel; Berserk supersedes Fanaticism; missile spells have exclusions/precedence; Truth Seeker excludes other spell bonuses. Choosing which effect is active manually can avoid a broad conflict engine while retaining faithful rules.
- **Next use remains manual by default.** Hinder, Truth Seeker and Speedart can offer their supported numeric subsets through matching conditional choices; Multimissile/Sureshot retain their manual attack workflows. All require relevant use/firing and explicit clearing. A named skill alone is not sufficient evidence to consume an effect on any test; none of these rows assumes consumption hooks.
- **Senses, terrain, control and auras require judgments beyond numbers.** Invisibility imposes a penalty on observers, Radiant Appearance on certain attackers, Protective Sphere depends on attack origin, and Care shares only the caster's named protections. Do not implement these as unconditional modifiers on the affected actor or automatic transfer from spell ownership.
- **Enchantment creation is not passive application.** Charms use wielder casting/MP; matrices use original enchanter skill and wielder MP; potions have distinct no-drinker-MP and bundled-effect rules; Divine items use religion/recharge. Their rows remain manual here while the enchanted-item ticket chooses the supported workflow.
