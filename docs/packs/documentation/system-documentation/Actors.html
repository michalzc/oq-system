Player Character

The header holds the portrait (click it to change the image) and the character's name, gender, age, culture, rank, organisation and concept.

Characteristics

The panel shows the final value of each characteristic: base plus mod. The gear icon opens the Characteristics dialogue:

  • Each row has the base value, a roll formula with a dice icon, and a mod. The Roll All icon rolls every characteristic. Rolls are posted to chat and fill in the base values.
  • The points section follows the rules: Spent is the sum of base values minus 56, Remain is the points available minus the spent ones. Points available default to 30.
  • Nothing is saved until you click Save Data. Reset Data restores the current values.

Mod is a simple way to track temporary changes: it is added to the base value.

Attributes

Attributes are calculated from characteristics and items:

  • Hit Points: (SIZ + CON) / 2, rounded, plus mod.
  • Magic Points: POW plus mod.
  • Damage Modifier: from STR + SIZ. Up to 10: -1d6, up to 15: -1d4, up to 25: none, up to 30: +1d4, then +1d6 for 31-45, +2d6 for 46-60, and so on. The mod is text, for example +1d4 or 2, and is appended to the result.
  • Movement Rate: 15 plus mod.
  • Armour Worn: the highest Armour Points among armour in the Worn or Natural state, plus mod. Armour does not stack.

The current Hit Points and Magic Points can be typed in, or adjusted by clicking the HP or MP label: left-click adds one, right-click removes one. Characters also track Fortune Points and Growth Points. The gear icon opens the Attributes dialogue, where only the mods can be changed.

Tabs

  • Skills: skills grouped by type, custom skill groups, and all special abilities. Group headers show the total of advances.
  • Combat: initiative, combat skills each followed by the weapons that use it, ammunition, resistances, armour and combat abilities. A weapon whose skill the character does not have is not listed here; it is still on the Equipment tab.
  • Equipment: money, total encumbrance, weapons, armour and other equipment.
  • Magic: magic skills, resistances, magic abilities, and spells grouped by type, with custom spell groups last. See the Magic page.
  • Notes: free text.

Working with items

  • Click an item's icon to send it to chat. Hover over its name to see the description and traits.
  • Click a name or value to roll a test, or a damage formula to roll damage. See the Rolls page.
  • The + button in a group header creates a new item of that type and opens its sheet.
  • The edit icon opens the item sheet. The trash icon deletes the item immediately, without confirmation.
  • Skill advances can be edited directly in the list. Typing a negative number sets the skill total instead: -65 makes the skill 65%.
  • Drop items from the sidebar or a compendium onto the sheet. Dropping a whole item folder adds every item in it, including subfolders.

Equipment and money

Weapons, armour and equipment have a state, shown as an icon in the Equipment tab. Click the icon to change it: weapons can be Readied, Carried, Stored or Natural; armour Worn, Carried, Stored or Natural; equipment Readied, Carried or Stored.

Total Encumbrance counts the items that are Worn, Readied or Carried; ammunition and consumables count once per unit. It is shown against the limit of STR + SIZ. No penalties are applied automatically.

Money has one field per coin from the Coins Configuration setting. Consolidate Coins exchanges all money into as few coins as possible. Item costs are shown in coins, for example 1GD 5SP.

NPC

The NPC sheet is a compact version of the character sheet. The header holds the name and a short description; the edit icon next to it opens an editor for that description.

Characteristics

Characteristics are edited directly on the sheet. Under each value is a roll formula, by default that of an average human: 3d6, or 2d6+6 for SIZ and INT. The dice icon rolls all formulas and updates the values, without a chat message.

Attributes

Attributes work as for characters, except that NPCs have no Fortune or Growth Points.

Tabs

  • Details: skills and general abilities, magic (magic skills, abilities and spells, followed by the custom spell groups), equipment with quantities, initiative, and a combat section with resistances, weapons, combat abilities and armour.
  • Description: free text.

NPC sheets have no add buttons: drop items onto the sheet. There is no money or encumbrance tracking.

Initiative

The Initiative field selects a skill or special ability with a formula, and the Mod field adds a whole number to it. The result is the actor's initiative; no dice are rolled. The same values are edited from the combat tracker; see the Combat page.

Roll data

Actors provide values for roll formulas, in skill formulas, damage formulas and chat commands:

  • @str, @con, @siz, @dex, @int, @pow, @cha: final characteristic values.
  • @dm: the damage modifier. It already includes its sign, so write 1d6 @dm, not 1d6 + @dm.
  • @skills.<slug>.value and @skills.<slug>.mod: a skill's total and mod, for example @skills.close-combat.value.
  • Other actor data by its path, for example @attributes.hp.value, @attributes.mp.max or @attributes.ap.value.
