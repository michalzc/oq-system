Tests

Click a skill, weapon or special ability name, or its value, on the actor sheet to open the roll dialogue. Shift-click rolls immediately, with Normal difficulty and the item's mod.

The dialogue shows the value and lets you choose:

  • Difficulty: Easy (+50%), Simple (+20%), Normal (0%), Difficult (-20%) or Hard (-50%). These are the OpenQuest SRD values; the GM can change them, see Rule Options below.
  • Other Mods: pre-filled with the skill's mod, or the weapon's combined mod; see the Items page.
  • Visibility: who can see the roll, defaulting to the mode selected in the chat log.

Results

This section describes the default OpenQuest SRD rules; Rule Options below lists what the GM can change.

The target (the Modified skill) is the value plus difficulty plus other mods, between 0 and 100 (with no upper limit when the Skills Capped at 100% Rule Option is off). Roll d100:

  • Critical Success: a double (11, 22 ... 99) at or under the target.
  • Success: any other roll at or under the target.
  • Failure: a roll over the target.
  • Fumble: a double over the target, or 00 (100).

When the value is 100 or more and the target is still 100 or more, the character is a Master: the result is always a Critical Success. The sheet marks the skill and the chat card says Mastered.

Rule Options

The GM can run the game with rules that differ from the OpenQuest SRD. Open Game Settings in the sidebar, go to the OQ System tab and click Configure Rule Options. Players do not see this menu. The options apply to every actor and every test: skills, special abilities, weapons and spells.

The Rules Preset box at the top shows OpenQuest SRD while every option has its SRD value, and Custom otherwise. Choosing OpenQuest SRD restores all SRD values; changing any option switches it to Custom.

Option OpenQuest SRD Alternative
Skills Capped at 100% on: skills, weapons and the target stop at 100% off: they can grow above 100%
Critical Success successful doubles a roll up to one tenth of the target, rounded up
Fumble failed doubles or a roll of 100 a roll of 100 only
Mastery on off: nobody is a Master
Easy Modifier +50 0 to 100
Simple Modifier +20 0 to 100
Difficult Modifier -20 -100 to 0
Hard Modifier -50 -100 to 0

The options can be combined freely:

  • Without the cap, the part of a value or target above 100 has no further effect: it does not reduce an opponent's skill. A target of 0 or less still always fails.
  • With the one-tenth critical rule, a target of 5 is a critical on 01 and a target of 27 on 01-03; doubles mean nothing on a success. A target of 0 or less has no critical range.
  • A roll of 100 is always a fumble, whatever the target, unless the character is a Master and Mastery is on.
  • With Mastery off, skills at 100% or more are rolled like any other: a success unless the roll is a fumble, with criticals by the Critical Success option.
  • The roll dialogue shows the current difficulty modifiers. Normal is always 0.

Saving changed options asks to reload the world; the new rules apply only after the reload. Chat cards already posted keep their results.

Damage

Click a damage formula on the actor sheet to roll damage immediately. Shift-click opens a dialogue where you can edit the formula and choose the visibility.

When a test with an item that has damage succeeds, the chat card has a Roll Damage button. It opens the damage dialogue for the owners of the actor.

Damage cards have two buttons that apply the damage to the selected tokens: Damage subtracts the target's Armour Worn, Damage I/A ignores armour. See the Chat page.
