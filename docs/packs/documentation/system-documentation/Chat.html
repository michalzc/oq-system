Chat Commands

  • /hp <roll formula>: rolls the formula and posts an Adjust Hit Points card with three buttons: Apply damage, Apply damage and ignore armour and Heal.
  • /mp <roll formula>: rolls the formula and posts an Adjust Magic Points card with Decrease Magic Points and Increase Magic Points buttons.

Formulas can use the roll data of your selected token's actor, or of your character, for example /hp 1d8 @dm. The card uses the visibility selected in the chat log.

Applying Changes

The buttons on /hp, /mp and damage roll cards apply the rolled amount to every selected token, not to targeted ones.

  • Damage subtracts the token actor's Armour Worn first; the ignore armour variant does not. What armour lets through takes temporary Hit Points first (see the Actors page), emptying the oldest pool before the next, then the ordinary Hit Points. Hit Points never drop below 0.
  • Healing restores ordinary Hit Points only, up to the maximum. It never refills temporary Hit Points.
  • Magic points never go above the maximum or below 0.

Each change is reported in chat with the points actually changed:

  • "Orc has taken 5 points of damage." when only ordinary Hit Points were lost.
  • "Orc has taken 7 points of damage, 4 of them absorbed by temporary hit points." when temporary Hit Points took part of it: here the Orc lost 4 temporary and 3 ordinary Hit Points.
  • "Orc soaked all damage." when armour stopped it completely; temporary Hit Points are then untouched.
  • "Orc has gained 3 hit points." for healing, counting only the points restored: an Orc at full Hit Points gains 0.

For example, an Orc with 6 of 10 Hit Points and two temporary pools, 4 then 3, takes 5 damage that ignores armour: the first pool is emptied and the second drops to 2, while its ordinary Hit Points stay at 6.
