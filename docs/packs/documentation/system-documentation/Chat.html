Chat Commands

  • /hp <roll formula>: rolls the formula and posts an Adjust Hit Points card with three buttons: Apply damage, Apply damage and ignore armour and Heal.
  • /mp <roll formula>: rolls the formula and posts an Adjust Magic Points card with Decrease Magic Points and Increase Magic Points buttons.

Formulas can use the roll data of your selected token's actor, or of your character, for example /hp 1d8 @dm. The card uses the visibility selected in the chat log.

Applying Changes

The buttons on /hp, /mp and damage roll cards apply the rolled amount to every selected token, not to targeted ones.

  • Damage subtracts the token actor's Armour Worn first; the ignore armour variant does not. Hit Points never drop below 0.
  • Healing and magic points never go above the maximum, and magic points never below 0.

Each change is reported in chat, for example "Orc has taken 5 points of damage.", or "Orc soaked all damage." when armour stopped it completely.
