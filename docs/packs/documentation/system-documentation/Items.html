Common

Every item sheet has an image, a name, the fields of its type, and a description. Weapons, equipment, spells and special abilities also have traits: type one or more, separated by commas, and remove them with their delete icon.

Weapons and spells refer to a skill by its slug: a simplified name shown under the skill's name, for example close-combat. On an item that no actor owns, the skill field is a text input for the slug. Once the item is on an actor, it becomes a list of the actor's skills (only magic skills for spells), and the skill with the entered slug is selected.

GMs can mark items that no actor owns as defaults for new actors, with Default for New Actors in the header menu. See the Getting Started page.

Skills

  • Type: Resistances, Combat, Knowledge, Practical or Magic Skills, or Custom Type with a group name of your choice. The type decides where the skill appears on actor sheets.
  • Formula: the base value, calculated from the actor's roll data. For example, @str+@dex is the sum of Strength and Dexterity.

On an actor, the skill also has Advances, Mod and Total Value. The total is the formula plus advances, between 0 and 100; at 100 the skill is Mastered. Advances can also be edited in the actor's skill list.

The mod is not added to the total. It is shown in brackets on the actor sheet and is pre-filled as Other Mods in the roll dialogue, where it can be changed before rolling.

Weapons

  • Hands: One Handed, Two Handed, or One or Two Handed.
  • Weapon Type: Melee, Ranged or Shield. Ranged weapons also have Range and Fire Rate, both free text.
  • Encumbrance, Cost and State.
  • Skill and Mod: the skill used to attack and an extra modifier. The weapon's value is the skill's total. Its mod is not added to the value: the roll dialogue receives the more significant of the weapon's and the skill's mods, following the rules.
  • Damage: a roll formula, which may use the actor's roll data. With Include DM ticked, the actor's damage modifier is added automatically.

A weapon can be rolled, and is listed in the Combat tab, only when the actor has its skill. Ammunition is a type of equipment and is not consumed automatically.

Armour

Armour Points, Encumbrance, Cost and State (Worn, Carried, Stored or Natural). The actor's Armour Worn uses the best piece that is Worn or Natural.

Equipment

  • Type: Ammunition, Consumables or Items.
  • Quantity, for ammunition and consumables. It can be changed with the +/- buttons on the actor sheet. Encumbrance is multiplied by quantity.
  • Encumbrance, Cost and State (Readied, Carried or Stored).

Special Abilities

  • Type: General, Magic or Combat. The type decides where the ability appears on actor sheets; all abilities are also listed on the character's Skills tab.
  • Formula: like a skill formula, using the actor's roll data. There are no advances or mod. The ability can be rolled only if the formula gives a fixed number, without dice.
  • Damage Formula: rolled like weapon damage, but the damage modifier is not added automatically.

Special abilities are useful for NPCs and creatures, but characters can have them too.

Hotbar macros

Drag a skill, weapon or special ability from an actor sheet to the hotbar to create a macro. The macro rolls the item with that name for the selected token's actor, or for your character, and always opens the roll dialogue.
