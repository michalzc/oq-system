Spells

  • Spell Type: Personal Magic, Divine Magic or Sorcery. Each type has its own icon and its own group on the actor's Magic tab. Custom Type takes a group name of your choice: custom spells are grouped by that name after the other spells, on the Magic tab and in the NPC spell list.
  • Magnitude.
  • Non-Variable: the spell is always cast at its full magnitude.
  • No Magic Points: casting needs no test and no magic points; the magnitude is used up instead. Divine Magic spells in the Spells compendium are set this way.
  • Skill: the magic skill used to cast the spell.

Tables referred to by spell descriptions, such as Fear Effects or Illusions, are in the @UUID[Compendium.oq.oq-system-documentation.JournalEntry.tQAx53vXPFGozPEk]{Spells Results} journal.

Casting

Click a spell's name on the actor sheet to cast it. Shift-click casts at the highest magnitude without asking.

Spells using magic points

The cast dialogue shows the casting skill's value, the Spell Magnitude (for variable spells, from 1 up to the magnitude or the actor's magic points, whichever is lower), Difficulty, Other Mods and Visibility. The spell needs a casting skill and enough magic points: one for variable spells, the full magnitude for non-variable ones.

The casting skill is tested as described on the Rolls page, and magic points are deducted automatically:

  • Success or Fumble: the full magnitude.
  • Critical Success or Failure: one magic point.

The chat card shows the magnitude, skill value, modifiers, the result and the magic points spent.

Spells without magic points

No test is rolled. A variable spell asks for the magnitude to use, up to what remains, and the remaining magnitude is reduced; the actor sheet shows it as remaining/magnitude. A non-variable spell is marked Expended after casting. A spell with nothing left cannot be cast.

The Regain spell icon restores a single spell. Regain all spells, in the spell group header, restores every spell of that type, or of that custom group; in the Magic group of NPC sheets it restores all spells.
